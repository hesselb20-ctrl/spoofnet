The idea

Maps today see the sky

Public interference maps are built from aircraft reports. Ports, shipping lanes, roads and fields stay blank.

Phones already listen

Every Android phone reports signal strength per satellite, receiver gain and clock. That is enough to tell a jammer from a roof.

Detection stays on the phone

Only events and coarse map cells are uploaded. Tracks never leave the device.

No data is never "clean"

Where there are no phones, the map says so, and every incident shows its confidence and coverage.

Where it stands, November 2026

  • Works in software replayDetectors, multi-phone localisation, API and map, measured on simulated phones.
  • One real observationOn a public log of one flight, the detector's single alarm matches the aircraft-based map cell by cell.
  • Not yet validated at ground or sea levelNo detection rate can be quoted from real data.
  • Phone app written, not yet built or field-testedThere are no contributors and no live data.
